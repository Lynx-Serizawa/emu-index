03 / Field notes

Before you
press start.

Emulation is legal in most places; sourcing games is the part that needs care. Here is the short version.

01

Dump your own media

Own the cartridge or disc? Rip it yourself. Downloading ROMs you do not own is copyright infringement in most jurisdictions — and it is not what this archive is for.

Ethics
02

Respect the BIOS

Several systems need a firmware dump to boot. Extract it from your own hardware. Emulator projects generally cannot and will not supply those files for you.

Firmware
03

Start with the pick

The highlighted emulator is the best balance of accuracy, usability and momentum. Chasing cycle-perfect emulation is fun — but it is a hobby, not a prerequisite.
